# Academic/Scientific Manuscript (academic-manuscript-skill)

## Overview
Formats documents according to formal academic journal submission guidelines (APA/IEEE). Implements IMRAD structural hierarchy, publication-grade Booktabs three-line tables without vertical borders, academic citations, and table/figure captions.

## Core Capabilities
1. **IMRAD Section Hierarchy**: Formats numbered sections: 1. Introduction, 2. Materials & Methods, 3. Results, 4. Discussion, 5. Conclusion, References.
2. **Booktabs Three-Line Tables**: Generates publication tables with heavy top border (1.5 pt), header separator border (0.75 pt), heavy bottom border (1.5 pt), and zero vertical lines.
3. **Formal In-Text Citations**: Preserves and standardizes citation formats (`[1]`, `[1-3]`, or `Author et al., Year`).
4. **Figure & Table Captions**: Formats `**Table 1:** *Descriptive Title*` above tables, and `**Figure 1:** *Descriptive Legend*` below figures.
5. **Abstract & Keywords Presentation**: Indented and styled abstract summaries and keyword indexing.
6. **Academic Typography**: 1.0-inch margins, 1.15x line spacing, running header, and page numbering.
